A forward problem you can inspect

Two rates can make one smooth recovery.

Change a known two-component model: M(t) = 1 − w exp(−Rslow t) − (1−w) exp(−Rfast t). This is a saturation-style q = 1 teaching kernel, with no noise. It does not perform inverse-Laplace analysis.

Slow and fast exponential recoveries form a smooth weighted sum.
  • Green: slow recovery
  • Orange dashed: fast recovery
  • Purple: weighted sum

RCOG = exp[w ln Rslow + (1−w) ln Rfast] for these log-rate bin weights; the arithmetic rate mean is a different quantity. Nuclear preparation and pulse sequence determine the appropriate real kernel.
