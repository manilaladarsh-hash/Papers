YBCO · 1991 · experiment

Cu and oxygen see different spin fluctuations

Cu and O share a decreasing uniform spin response but filter momentum differently; the Cu relaxation maximum alone does not require a new gap opening at 150 K.

YBCO · 2002 · experiment

Ca and oxygen disentangle holes and disorder

At oxygen content near 6.0, Ca-doped YBCO shows growing low-frequency Cu fluctuations despite a decreasing uniform response; oxygen loading restores a spin-gap-like relaxation signature.

Extra anisotropy on an orthorhombic background

In-plane torque reveals additional susceptibility anisotropy near the pseudogap onset, beyond the background already imposed by YBCO chains.

An ultrafast magnetic test of the driven state
