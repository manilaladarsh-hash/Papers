Keep the units visible

Every frame spends part of the dose budget.

Hashimoto’s 50 V, 5 pA example uses about 1.2 × 10⁻⁶ C/cm² per frame. This explorer counts exposure using that reported frame dose; it does not predict chemical damage or a new specimen’s tolerance.

Cumulative charge relative to the reported 1.4 × 10⁻⁵ C/cm² tolerance

D = N Dframe. RNCC’s 0.85 cutoff describes this paper’s image-comparison criterion. Frame dose is rounded; an arithmetic threshold cannot reproduce an exact micrograph or resolve charging.
